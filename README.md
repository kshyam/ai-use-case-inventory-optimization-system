## Overview

**Inventory Optimization System** is a reference AI workflow for Retail & E-commerce organizations. It addresses a practical supply chain challenge:

> Merchandising and planning teams balance demand, promotions, supply and inventory across disconnected signals.

The workflow is triggered when **Scheduled forecast or supply disruption**. It coordinates Demand forecast, inventory and supplier agents and keeps people responsible for decisions that require judgment, exception handling, or a sensitive business action.

## Why This AI Workflow Matters

Organizations often have the data and systems needed to solve this problem, but the information is spread across teams and operational tools. An AI agent workflow can help bring the right context together, explain the next step, and carry out approved actions without hiding the decision trail.

This is an architecture pattern, not a promise of automatic results. Production implementation depends on data quality, system access, workflow evaluation, security controls, and a clear human review design.

## How It Works

1. **Combine demand and promotion signals**
2. **Forecast demand**
3. **Detect inventory risk**
4. **Recommend replenishment**
5. **Coordinate suppliers after planner approval**

## AI Agents Involved

Demand forecast, inventory and supplier agents.

## Human Oversight and Security Controls

**Human checkpoint:** Approve replenishment plans and supplier commitments.

Recommended controls for this AI agent use case:

- Planner permissions
- Scenario evidence
- ERP write approval
- Supplier access policy

These controls define what the AI system can see, recommend, or do, and when a person must review the outcome. High-impact actions should fail closed when identity, policy, or approval information is missing.
